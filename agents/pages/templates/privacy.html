Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name. We use these to identify your account and address you by name. We do not receive your Google password.

We store the trip itineraries, notes, and files you create or import. This data is stored in our database and is associated with your account.

How we use it

Your email is used to identify your account and, if you request it, to send a password-reset link. We do not send marketing email. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Trip data you mark as public is accessible to anyone with the link. Trips you do not publish remain private to your account.

Third-party services

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Google OAuth - for sign-in. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic Claude - to parse and process trip content you upload or type. Text you submit may be sent to Anthropic's API.
  • Render - our hosting provider. Your data is stored on Render's infrastructure.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap - for geocoding trip locations to show them on a map.

Data retention and deletion

Your account and all associated trips remain until you delete them or ask us to remove them. To request deletion, email us at the address below.
